### apy

- **Source URL:** https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/apy

#### English (en)
- **Official name:** Atal Pension Yojana
- **Card name:** Atal Pension Yojana
- **Summary:** Atal Pension Yojana guarantees a monthly pension of Rs 1,000‑5,000 from age 60 for life, extends the same amount to the spouse after death, and returns the accumulated corpus to the nominee, while contributions enjoy tax relief.
- **Benefit:** The scheme gives a guaranteed minimum pension of Rs. 1000, Rs. 2000, Rs. 3000, Rs. 4000 or Rs. 5000 per month after age 60 until death. The spouse receives the same pension after the subscriber’s death. The nominee gets the accumulated pension wealth after both die.
- **Who can apply:** People aged 18 to 40 can join. There is no income requirement. Citizens who are or have been income tax payers are not eligible.
- **Documents:** Active bank or post office savings account for KYC, Aadhaar for identity verification, and Aadhaar virtual ID or XML file for KYC upload. The CSC centre will tell you the full list of papers.
- **How to apply:** Visit the eNPS website and select Atal Pension Yojana. Fill basic and nominee details, choose the pension amount and contribution frequency, give consent for auto‑debit, and complete Aadhaar KYC by OTP or uploading the XML/Virtual ID.

### day-nrlm

- **Source URL:** https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/day-nrlm

#### English (en)
- **Official name:** Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana - National Rural Livelihoods Mission
- **Card name:** Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana - National Rural Livelihoods Mission
- **Summary:** The scheme links rural poor households to women-led Self Help Groups, offering bank‑linked credit, revolving and community investment funds, interest subvention, skill training, and market support to boost livelihoods and financial inclusion.
- **Benefit:** The scheme brings one member, preferably a woman, from each rural poor household into a Self Help Group network. Women SHGs get bank linkage, capacity building, livelihood promotion and financial inclusion. Revolving funds and interest subvention support SHG credit.
- **Who can apply:** Self Help Groups that have been active for at least six months and follow regular meetings, savings, inter‑loaning, timely repayment and up‑to‑date accounts are eligible. Groups graded by NABARD standards qualify. Defunct groups can join after reviving for three months.
- **Documents:** Aadhar number, proof of identity, proof of residence, voter ID card, passport size photo are required. The CSC centre will tell you the full list of papers.
- **How to apply:** Visit the DAY‑NRLM registration portal online. Fill mandatory fields such as name, email and contact number. Provide a username, create and confirm a password, then click Create New Account.

### igndps

- **Source URL:** https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/igndps

#### English (en)
- **Official name:** Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme
- **Card name:** Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme
- **Summary:** The Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme provides Rs 300 monthly to Divyangjan aged 18 to 79 and Rs 500 monthly after 80, for Indian residents with over 80 percent disability from Below Poverty Line families.
- **Benefit:** The scheme pays Rs.300/- per month to disabled people between 18 and 79 years. The scheme pays Rs.500/- per month to disabled people aged 80 years and above.
- **Who can apply:** People aged 18 years and above can apply. Applicants are residents of India with disability more than 80%. Applicants belong to Below Poverty Line and dwarfs are also included.
- **Documents:** Papers needed are BPL Card and Aadhaar card and disability certificate. Age proof and passport size photographs are also needed. The CSC centre will tell you the full list of papers.
- **How to apply:** People apply online through UMANG App and search for NSAP. People fill basic details and choose mode of payment of pension. People upload photo and submit the form.
