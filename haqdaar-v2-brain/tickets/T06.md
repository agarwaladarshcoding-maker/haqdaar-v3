# T06 · What one scheme record contains

**Type:** `wayfinder:grilling` (HITL) · **Module:** Data · **Blocked by:** T02 · **Blocks:** T07, T08, T09, T16

## Question

What is the exact shape of one scheme in our store, and how is eligibility represented?

To resolve:

- **Tags vs rules** — the decision deferred since 13 Aug. Tags are cheap and fuzzy; rules are precise and expensive to author 50 times.
- The closed vocabulary of eligibility dimensions (state, age band, occupation, category, land, gender, income...) and how a scheme says "doesn't care about this one".
- How the four read-back chunks are stored, and their length ceiling in spoken seconds.
- Provenance: source URL, capture date, who verified it, whether it is safe to speak.
- What happens to a scheme whose criteria we cannot express — dropped, or spoken with a caveat?

## Why it blocks

This is the spine. The engine filters it, the model routes to it, the Mouth reads it, the LOG cites it.

## Default if this stalls

Closed-vocabulary tags with an explicit UNKNOWN, plus a small rules escape hatch for numeric bands only.

---

## Resolution — 29 Aug 2026

**Closed.** The record is one row in a flat, hand-captured table, validated at build, expanded into bitmasks at boot. Eligibility is **facets, not rules**.

### The shape

Five field groups. One row per scheme. CSV is the capture format; the build reads it and nothing else.

**A · Identity and provenance**

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| `id` | 1..N, capture order, never reused |
| `bit` | **assigned at build from row order, not captured.** Word width derives from row count |
| `myscheme_slug`, `source_url` | validator cross-checks slug against URL; URL must be a `myscheme.gov.in` scheme page |
| `level` | STATE · CENTRAL |
| `state`, `department` | the implementing body |
| `facets_verified` | YES only. See the tightened definition below |
| `verified_by`, `verified_on` | human name, date |

**B · Matching (Door A)**

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| `scheme_name_en` / `_hi` / `_mr` | the card title in each language |
| `alias_{lang}_1..3` | **what a caller actually says.** Not optional — a first-class field |

Aliases are a record field, not an enhancement. Nobody speaks a twelve-word official title, and matching against the title alone fails the caller who says half of it.

**C · Filtration — the mask columns**

Box 0 `category` is the load-bearing column: it is a property of the *scheme*, not the person, it is read off the caller's opening sentence, and it costs zero questions. Every downstream design (T09 mask table, T10 planner) assumes it is applied before question one.

Remaining columns are the person boxes plus `welfare_board` (derived from eligibility prose, no rail row exists) and `benefit_type`.

- Numerics (`age_min`, `age_max`, `disability_min_pct`) are stored **exact**. They become closed sets only at build, one mask per year / per point. No banding — banding would lose precision to buy nothing, since the mask table is precomputed anyway.
- `disability_min_pct` is conditional on `disabled = YES`. The **validator** owns that rule; the Filter never sees a conditional.
- Write-only columns (income, economic distress) are captured but generate no mask, because no facet exists to filter against.

**No column may be dropped on the evidence of the 16 Aug test report.** Every number there was measured against the inferred corpus that has since been dropped, and is explicitly shape, not fact. Minority scoring 0 and Government Employee scoring 0 are not grounds for deleting columns; that call is made once against the real captured corpus.

**D · Negative rules**

T02 established that **exclusions arrive as their own separate block** on the myScheme card, and `govt_employee = NO` already proves the record needs negative polarity.

- An exclusion that lands on an existing column is **subtracted from that column's admitted set at capture**. No second mechanism, no second code path in the Filter.
- An exclusion that lands on no column becomes an entry in `gate_notes` — **a list, not a single free-text field.** A scheme routinely carries several ("excludes anyone drawing another pension", "excludes holdings over two hectares").
- `gate_notes` is **never spoken and never filtered on.** It exists so the LOG can carry a scheme's declared blind spots. T16 cannot prove a choice is clean without it.

**E · Read-back**

Six chunks, each in three languages, all verbatim from the card except the first:

`summary` · `benefit_text` · `who_can_apply` · `documents` · `how_to_apply`

**`summary` is new and is the resolution of a real collision.** The acceptance bar requires a named scheme read back in under 20 seconds. `benefit_text` verbatim off a myScheme card is not 20 seconds in Marathi, and the capture rule forbids paraphrase. Both cannot hold. So: one authored, human-verified, ~35-word one-breath summary per language. Door A reads the summary, then offers the four verbatim sections by keypad. Without this field Door A misses the bar by construction.

The record stores **text**, never audio. Audio is derivative — content-addressed from (text, language, voice) at ingest per the corpus-snapshot decision. There is no audio column.

### Sentinel semantics

One sentinel: **`ANY`**. It means the scheme does not gate on this dimension.

Card silence maps to `ANY`, which is faithful to the source — myScheme's own search returns a facet-silent scheme for every value of that facet. The honesty of the whole corpus therefore rests entirely on `facets_verified`, which is **tightened here**:

> `facets_verified = YES` means a human read the eligibility prose *and* confirmed that the rail's silence on each `ANY` column is real silence, not an unread gate.

A second UNKNOWN sentinel was considered and rejected: it would let a half-captured row enter the corpus wearing a disclaimer. The build gate does that job better — a row that cannot be fully expressed does not enter the snapshot at all.

### A scheme whose criteria we cannot express

**Dropped, not spoken with a caveat.** The build refuses to start unless the validator passes with zero errors and `facets_verified = YES` on every row. No mask is generated and no audio is rendered from an unverified row. The demo corpus is hand-curated and deliberately small, so exclusion costs one row and buys the guarantee that nothing spoken is unverified.

### Seam — what T06 does not decide

- How verified content survives three languages, and who authors the summary → **T08**
- What a mask means and how survivors are computed → **T09**
- Freshness policy, re-capture triggers, what a stale row does → **T21**
- What the LOG carries from `gate_notes` and the mask trail → **T16**
- Which schemes, and the occupation / disability value lists → **T07**, which now also needs **T22**

### Graduated

- **T07 now additionally blocked by T22.** The `occupation` and `disability_min_pct` value lists were never recovered from the portal. The record's *shape* does not need them, so T06 closes without T22 — but no row can be captured against an unenumerated column, so T07 cannot start until T22 lands.
