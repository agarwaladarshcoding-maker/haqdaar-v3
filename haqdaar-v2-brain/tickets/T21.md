# ~~T21 · What the record says about provenance and freshness~~

**Type:** `wayfinder:grilling` · **Module:** Data · **Blocked by:** ~~T06~~ · **Blocks:** — · **Closed 10 Sep 2026 (rev 25)**

*Renumbered from T20 on 23 Aug — a parallel session had already taken that number for "Whether call audio may leave India".*

## Question

Every spoken line is a compression, written by us, of prose scraped from myScheme on some date. What does the scheme record store about that, and does the caller ever hear it?

Must settle:

- Whether each record carries a source URL, a fetch date, and a verified-by marker per language — or whether the corpus snapshot number alone is sufficient provenance.
- Whether the read-back ever voices a freshness or source caveat, and if so at which of the four chunks. Standing rule applies: never says "you are eligible".
- What the LOG records about content provenance. The acceptance bar demands it prove *why* each scheme was chosen — a filter question — but it is silent on whether the read-back text was current.
- Who re-verifies when a scheme's content drifts before 12 Sep, and what the record does with a scheme that fails verification.

## Why it matters

T02 established that no myScheme content arrives warranted, that non-English text is disclaimed by the platform itself, and that third-party datasets of it can be fabricated. A record with no provenance box cannot distinguish a verified line from an inherited one — and the three-language verification T08 designs has nowhere to write its result.

## Default if this stalls

Snapshot number plus source URL and fetch date on each record; a per-language `verified_by` and `verified_on` pair; no caveat is ever spoken to the caller.

---

## Resolution

**The default holds on the record and is overturned on the caller.** The fields survive nearly as written, with one addition that does real work (a hash). The *"no caveat is ever spoken"* clause was already dead before this session opened: T23's second pass (rev 24) put a source frame in front of every verbatim section. T21 ratifies that frame, pins where it may and may not go, and refuses the one caveat that remained — a spoken date. Freshness is enforced by a build gate instead of a sentence, which is the same move T18 made with the forbidden-phrase check: **on this map, what cannot be trusted to a caller's ear becomes a gate.**

### Branch 1 · Is the snapshot number enough provenance? No.

- **A snapshot's number says when it was built, not when its text was read.** Snapshots are immutable and content-addressed (rev 3, T15). A record fetched on 11 Sep can ride unchanged into a snapshot built on 20 Sep. The number would claim nine days of freshness the text does not have.
- **So provenance lives on the record.** Three fields, flat, in T06's identity/provenance group:

| Field | Holds | Why it earns its place |
|---|---|---|
| `source_url` | the myScheme page the English was captured from | the first question anyone asks; also the input to the host gate below |
| `fetched_on` | date our own scraper read the page | the only honest freshness fact we own |
| `source_sha256` | hash of the five captured English blocks (benefits, eligibility, documents, how-to-apply, exclusions), as fetched | **makes drift a comparison instead of a reading** — re-fetch, re-hash, diff. Without it, "has this changed?" needs a human to re-read every scheme |

- **`source_url` must be on `myscheme.gov.in`, fetched by our scraper.** T02 found a public third-party dataset that fabricates eligibility rules and Hindi text on fetch failure. A host check is the cheapest defence against inheriting it.

### Branch 2 · What does "verified" mean per language, when T08 staffs no verifier?

- **The pair survives, and it is allowed to be null.** Per language, per record:

| Field | Values |
|---|---|
| `{lang}_sections_origin` | `source` (the page's own words) · `machine` · `human` |
| `{lang}_summary_origin` | `human` · `machine` |
| `{lang}_verified_by` | a name, or null |
| `{lang}_verified_on` | a date, or null |

- **For 12 Sep the honest values are:** English sections `source`, Hindi and Marathi sections `machine`, and `verified_by` null for Hindi and Marathi.
- **A field that is null on every non-English row is still worth storing, for two reasons.**
  - T08 says nothing built for the demo may assume the truth lock stays suspended. Reinstating it should mean filling a field, not changing a schema.
  - A null puts the debt in the data itself, where anyone reading the snapshot sees it. Otherwise the debt lives only in the map, where no reader of the data will look.
- **One rule makes the null mean something:** if either origin is `human`, `verified_by` and `verified_on` are required. So a null can only ever mean "no human touched this". It can never mean "someone forgot to fill it in".
- **T06's `facets_verified` gains a who and a when** (`facets_verified_by`, `facets_verified_on`). Reading the eligibility prose to confirm every `ANY` is the one human verification act the demo keeps, so it is the one that most needs a name on it.

### Branch 3 · Does the caller ever hear a caveat? A source caveat, in one place. A freshness caveat, never.

- **Source caveat — ratified from T23 (rev 24):** `section_source_frame` plays before every verbatim section, and says *translated* in Hindi and Marathi.
- **It must never precede `summary`.** The summary is our compression, in our voice, under the first tier of T18's gate. Attributing it to "the official page" would itself be an untruth. That is the one constraint this branch adds to Engine's sequencing.
- **Not before names, terminals or nearest schemes either.** Nearest reads `summary` only (T18), so it never meets the frame at all.
- **Freshness caveat — refused. The caller never hears a date.**
  - **It isn't a fact about the scheme.** `fetched_on` says when we read the page, not that the page is still true. *"As of 11 September"* sounds like a guarantee and isn't one.
  - **The caller can't act on it.** A feature-phone caller has no way to check a date against anything.
  - **It costs audio that would be spoken without verification.** A spoken date is a per-scheme, per-language string — either a new chip family or a slot inside a sentence, which T15 still forbids. And T08 put no human in front of it.
- **Freshness becomes a gate instead of a sentence** (Branch 4).

### Branch 4 · Who re-verifies drift, and what happens to a scheme that fails?

- **The fourth build gate, same shape as the other three** (alias floor, six-chunk render, forbidden phrase): a record that fails it **does not enter the snapshot**. No partial record, no warning flag, no degraded read. It is the same rule T06 set for a row that cannot be fully expressed.
  - **Host:** `source_url` is on `myscheme.gov.in`.
  - **Age:** `fetched_on` is within `max_source_age_days`, which lives in `contracts/tunables.py` (T17, no ceremony). Default **14**. Every demo record will have been fetched in the same week, so this is a guard against a stale file sneaking in, not a demo constraint.
  - **Drift:** before the demo snapshot freezes, one command re-fetches every page and re-hashes. **A changed `source_sha256` excludes the record until its owner re-reads it.** Re-reading means: re-confirm the facets, re-author the summary, re-run the three other gates. There is no auto-accept, because the hash can tell *that* the text changed but not *whether it matters*.
- **Who:** the Data owner, or the Data+Engine owner if the team runs under four (T17). The drift check runs **once, on the evening before the demo snapshot freezes**, which shrinks the fetch-to-demo window to under a day.
- **If drift knocks out one of the fixture's five coverage schemes** (T17: match, soft-miss, hard-miss, near-`ANY`, alias pair), hand re-read that one rather than lose the coverage role — about 20 minutes a scheme. Any other drifted scheme simply leaves the snapshot.
- **After the snapshot freezes, drift is not our problem for that session.** The call is pinned to its snapshot (rev 3), and `fetched_on` says exactly what was read and when.

### Branch 5 · What does the LOG record about provenance? Nothing new — and that creates one obligation.

- **No new LOG field.** T16's rule applies: the snapshot id (already written at call open) plus the scheme ids and chunks spoken already reconstruct provenance. `source_url`, `fetched_on`, hash and verification all sit on the record in that snapshot. Copying them into every line would be storing what can be derived.
- **The obligation:** "derive, don't store" only works if the thing you derive from outlives the log that cites it. **So snapshot records and manifests are kept at least as long as any LOG that names them** — committed to the repo, since they are small text.
  - **The ~200 MB audio pool stays sweepable** (T15), because the LOG explains rather than replays and never needed the audio.
  - **T15's mark-and-sweep is amended by one line:** the sweep may delete audio, never records.

### Branch 6 · T22's handoff — where did the occupation vocabulary come from?

- T22 recorded a provenance cost: the occupation list is cut from the corpus's own eligibility prose, not taken from the portal, and that is "T21's question".
- **Answered at snapshot level, not record level:** the snapshot manifest carries `vocab_source` per box. For 12 Sep, `occupation` is `corpus_prose_cut`, and boxes whose sets were authored from T10's roster are `authored`. One value per box per snapshot; no record changes.

## What moved

- **T06 amended.** The identity/provenance group gains `source_url`, `fetched_on` and `source_sha256`, plus per-language origin and verification fields. `facets_verified` gains a who and a when.
- **T15 amended.** The sweep deletes audio, never records.
- **T16 unchanged** — no new field, but snapshot retention is now a stated obligation.
- **T17.** `max_source_age_days` joins `tunables.py`. The snapshot layout is Data-internal for these fields: the Engine reads none of them, so no edge is crossed.
- **T18.** The fourth gate is added, in the same shape as the other three.
- **T23 ratified.** The source frame goes before verbatim sections only, never before `summary`.
- **T22's provenance handoff closed.**

## Handoffs

- **Data (T07).** Scraper writes `source_url`, `fetched_on`, `source_sha256` at capture. Ingest sets origin and verification fields honestly — null where no human read. Build runs the fourth gate. `make refetch-check` exists before the demo snapshot freezes. Manifest carries `vocab_source`. Records and manifests are committed.
- **Engine.** `section_source_frame` precedes verbatim sections and never `summary`.
- **Audio.** Nothing.
- **Judges.** Provenance for any spoken scheme is: open the LOG, take the snapshot id, open that snapshot's record, read five fields.
