# ~~T23 · What the confirmation ladder runs on now that there is no ASR confidence~~

**Type:** `wayfinder:grilling` · **Module:** Model · **Blocked by:** ~~T09~~, ~~T10~~, ~~T11~~ · **Blocks:** — · **Closed 10 Sep 2026** · **Amended 10 Sep, second pass (rev 24) — see §5**

## Question

Now that confidence numbers have been eliminated from ASR (T03) and LLM selection (T11), what exact sentences, templates, and affordances drive turn-level confirmation, fallback, and read-back navigation across Hindi, English, and Marathi?

To resolve:
- **Confirmation ladder mechanics.** With no confidence threshold (the 0.50 / 0.85 gates eliminated by T03 and T11), what exact turn structure replaces it?
- **The full fixed line set.** Enumerate the complete inventory of template lines, with unique string IDs, grouped by functional role.
- **The two carve-outs.** Codify the unwritten `state` cardinality > 9 → `UNKNOWN` rule with its spoken disclaimer, and author the 6 per-box rephrase lines implied by T11's ask-counter.
- **The bad-news-first ordering lock & build gate.** Assert that qualifying negative clauses strictly precede scheme names in all non-exact terminals, and verify zero prohibited words ("eligible", "qualify", "entitled", "you will get", "you can get") across all languages.
- **Sealing conversational holes.** Codify zero-runtime-TTS assembly for opener bundle confirmation, Door A candidate disambiguation, Door A downgrade to Door B, and multi-scheme read-back cycling.

## Why it matters

The acceptance bar demands real callers on 8 kHz feature phones navigate scheme discovery in under 20 s and reach trusted terminal states. Because T08 suspended human verification of translated text, the machine build gate and structural ordering rules are the sole guards preventing false eligibility promises.

---

## Resolution

T23 is pure authoring. Everything upstream is closed: T03 removed ASR confidence, T11 eliminated LLM confidence and established unconditional echo-confirm, T12 exempted Door A scheme names from confirmation, T14 set the turn clocks and `#` repeat key, T15 pinned zero runtime TTS, T17 froze line IDs as manifest-resolved strings, and T18 established the bad-news-first ordering lock across three terminals.

Stress-testing the end-to-end IVR lifecycle reveals 4 open conversational holes where the engine lacked audio to speak. Addressing them expanded the inventory from $N = 38$ to what the first pass filed as $N = 41$ ($3N + 1 = 124$) — **but the tables it filed number 42 lines, so the true first-pass figure was 127 files.** The second pass (§5) found the state machine was not yet sealed: it adds five lines, merges one away, and settles at **$N = 46$, $3N + 1 = 139$ files**, plus one value-chip family that replaces the per-value carrier family.

### 1 · The zero-confidence confirmation ladder

Confidence scores are replaced by mechanical turn structure:
- **Unconditional Echo-Confirm for Spoken Answers (T11), assembled from chips (rev 24):** Spoken input costs 2 turns; keypad costs 1 turn (T10). Every spoken value is echoed as `say([bundle_confirm_intro, val_<box>_<value>, confirm_yn_suffix])` — *"You mentioned: female. Press 1 for yes, or press 2 for no."* — the opener bundle's shape with one chip. **The per-value carrier family `confirm_<box>_<value>` (~600 files) is struck.** The first pass needed bare chips for the bundle *and* carriers for single confirms — two per-value families for one job. One bare chip per (box, value, language) now serves both, and `confirm_yn_suffix`, which the first pass authored and never used, becomes load-bearing.
- **Door A Exemption (T12):** Spoken scheme names are never confirmed prior to read-back. A wrong scheme is loud and self-correcting; keypad barge-in provides the correction.
- **Two-Turn Keypad Drop (T11):** Two consecutive non-`ANSWER` turns (`UNCLEAR`, `NOISE`, timeout, or mismatched span) on an askable box drop that box to its keypad menu (`keypad_<box>`).
- **Two-Turn Keypad Failure (T18):** Two non-`ANSWER`s on a box already on keypad drop that box to `UNKNOWN`.
- **Control Keys:**
  - `#` repeats the last spoken audio buffer via `repeat()` at any time. Costs 0 turns, 0 model calls, and consumes no silence ladder rungs (T14).
  - `*` initiates mid-call language re-pinning at any time (T24) by replaying `greeting_trilingual` — no new line.
  - **`0` always means *none of these*** (rev 24): *don't know / prefer not to say* on every box keypad menu, *neither* on Door A disambiguation. One meaning on every menu, so the caller learns it once.
- **Silence Ladder (Non-Read-Back Turns, T14):**
  - Rung 1 (6 s after the question's `playedStream`): replays the question audio buffer.
  - Rung 2 (6 s after rung 1's `playedStream`): speaks `silence_presence` (*"Are you still there? To hear the question again, press hash."*).
  - Rung 3 (6 s after rung 2's `playedStream`): speaks `closing_farewell`, waits its `playedStream`, and hangs up.
  - *(rev 24: the first pass wrote +6/+12/+18 s, which is only true if every replay is zero seconds long — each timer hangs off the previous line's `playedStream`, T14.)*
  - **Any keypress, control keys included, resets the ladder to rung 0** — the ladder measures presence and a keypress is presence. T14's 10-minute ceiling stays the only bound on a caller who presses `#` forever.

### 2 · The two carve-outs

#### A. Cardinality > 9 → `UNKNOWN` for `state` (and Keypad State Disclaimer)
- **Rule:** `state` has 36 values in India. Keypad menus cannot present 36 items without burning turns and cognitive clarity. Under T15, any failure on `state` drops directly to `UNKNOWN`. In call-level keypad-only mode (T18), `state` cannot be answered via keypad and is forced to `UNKNOWN`.
- **Spoken Disclaimer (`state_unknown_disclaimer`):** Spoken at the terminal *before* scheme names to announce that only nationwide schemes (`state: ANY`) are being evaluated:
  - **English:** *"Your state was not confirmed. Searching only schemes available across the whole country."*
  - **Hindi:** *"आपके राज्य की पुष्टि नहीं हुई। केवल पूरे देश में लागू योजनाएं खोजी जा रही हैं।"*
  - **Marathi:** *"तुमच्या राज्याची खात्री झाली नाही. फक्त संपूर्ण देशासाठी उपलब्ध योजना शोधल्या जात आहेत."*
- **Gate Check:** No claims of eligibility or entitlement. Explicitly bounds the geographical scope before reading names.

#### B. Per-Box Rephrase Forms (T11 Ask-Counter)
- **Rule:** When caller triggers `CLARIFY` (*"kya matlab?"*) or a box's ask-counter reaches 1, the system speaks an explanatory rephrase anchored in concrete examples:
  - `rephrase_state`: *"Please tell me the state or union territory where you stay."*
  - `rephrase_gender`: *"Are you a man or a woman?"*
  - `rephrase_social_category`: *"Which caste category do you belong to — General, OBC, SC, or ST?"*
  - `rephrase_age`: *"Please say your age in years."*
  - `rephrase_income_band`: *"Roughly how much does your entire household earn in one year?"*
  - `rephrase_occupation`: *"What is your main job — like farming, daily labour, or shopkeeping?"*

### 3 · Closed conversational edge cases

- **Opener Bundle Confirmation under Zero-Runtime-TTS:** Pre-rendering all combinations of states, categories, and occupations is combinatorially impossible. Resolved via a list-formatted sequence of pre-rendered keyword chips:
  `say([bundle_confirm_intro, val_<box>_<value>, …, confirm_yn_suffix])`
  *(rev 24: the first pass named a `bundle_confirm_outro` the inventory never held. The outro is `confirm_yn_suffix`, and `confirm_bundle_opener` is merged into it — T11 drops every opener box to UNASKED on "no" whichever wording asked, so two wordings bought nothing.)*
  **This amends T15's composition rule rather than following it.** T15 said never carrier-plus-slot. The honest rule is narrower: a slot *inside* a clause has agreement to break in Hindi and Marathi; a bare noun chip after a colon pause has none. Chips after a colon are allowed; slots inside a sentence stay forbidden.
- **Door A Two-Candidate Disambiguation:** Framed cleanly using terminal slots:
  `door_a_option_1` (*"I found two schemes. Press 1 for:"*) + `scheme_1.name` + `door_a_option_2` (*"Or press 2 for:"* ) + `scheme_2.name` + `door_a_option_none` (*"If neither, press 0."*).
  *(rev 24: the first pass left a caller whose scheme is neither with no key to press.)* `0` seeds Door B through `door_a_downgrade_to_b`, whose wording already fits.
- **Scheme Read-Back Navigation:** Added `next_scheme_intro` (*"Here is the next scheme:"*) and `no_more_schemes` (*"That was the last scheme."*) to handle transitions when pressing `9`. *(rev 24: the first wording, "Those are all the matching schemes", was false after an overflow — more matched — and false after a nearest — none matched.)* **A nearest terminal never plays `section_menu`** (T18: `summary` only, no sections): it auto-advances through at most two summaries, then `anything_else`.
- **Door A Downgrade Path:** Added `door_a_downgrade_to_b` to transition callers who named a scheme with $\ge 3$ matches into Door B questioning.

### 4 · The full template inventory ($N = 46$ after §5, Total Files = 139)

*Row numbers are display-only; the id is the identity (T17). Rows added by the second pass carry letter suffixes rather than renumbering the table.*

*Build Gate Compliance: Zero occurrences of "eligible", "qualify", "entitled", "you will get", "you can get", or Hindi/Marathi equivalents (पात्र, हकदार, मिलेगा, पा सकते हैं, पात्र, हक्क, मिळेल, मिळू शकते).*

*(rev 24) **The first pass's compliance claim was false**: `closing_farewell` says **हकदार** in Hindi and **हकदारला** in Marathi — the brand name is the forbidden word. The gate is corrected in two ways, neither of which weakens it:*
- *Brand allowlist: the exact token HAQDAAR / हकदार (and its Marathi inflection) is exempt **in `closing_farewell` only**. Thanking someone for calling a service is not a claim about the caller.*
- *Two tiers (amends T18): **our authored voice** — every fixed line and every `summary` — keeps the bare-word ban above. **Verbatim source sections** (benefits, how to apply, documents, who can apply) cannot pass it, because myScheme's own eligibility prose says "eligible" in nearly every scheme, and the six-chunk render gate (T15) would then exclude the whole corpus. Source sections are gated on **second-person claims only** (EN "you are eligible / you will get / you can get"; HI "आप पात्र / आपको मिलेगा"; MR "तुम्ही पात्र / तुम्हाला मिळेल"), which is the failure machine translation actually produces, and are always spoken behind `section_source_frame`.*

### 5 · Second pass (10 Sep, rev 24) — what the first pass got wrong

The first pass was authoring-complete and state-machine-incomplete. Walked against a call end to end, it had four kinds of defect: **arithmetic, lines that lie, callers with no key to press, and a gate that fails its own inventory.**

**Arithmetic**
- Tables numbered 42 lines, summary said 41, files said 124. True first-pass figure: 127.
- `bundle_confirm_outro` referenced, never authored. `confirm_yn_suffix` authored, never referenced.
- Handoff called the forbidden-phrase check the *fourth* build gate. It is T18's, and the third.
- Silence ladder written as cumulative +6/+12/+18 s; timers hang off each line's `playedStream`.

**Lines that told the caller something false**
- `unclear_prompt` — "after the tone". There is no tone; T14 ruled out every filler tone.
- `silence_presence` — "press any key". Digits are answers (T14) and `*` re-pins language: a caller obeying it could stamp a box or open the language menu. Now names `#`.
- `terminal_nearest_preamble` — "these two" when the gate can leave one.
- `no_more_schemes` — "all the matching schemes" after an overflow or a nearest.
- Hindi first-person verbs were masculine (*पाया, सकता हूँ, बताऊंगा*) and Marathi *शकतो* likewise, which **binds the render to a male voice** and defeats T15's cheap voice swap. Rewritten impersonal in every line that carried one.

**Callers with no key to press**
- No keypad route to `UNKNOWN` on any box menu → `keypad_unknown_suffix`, `0`.
- Door A disambiguation had no *neither* → `door_a_option_none`, `0`.
- Eligibility section rendered and gated but unreachable → key 4 on `section_menu`, behind `section_source_frame`.
- Consent line deferred to a build that cannot add audio → `consent_notice`.
- `rephrase_gender` dropped "other", narrowing the set the caller hears below the set the box holds.

**A gate that failed its own inventory**
- The brand name is the forbidden word → closing-only allowlist.
- A bare-word gate over verbatim sections excludes nearly every myScheme scheme → two tiers (amends T18).

**Structural**
- Per-value carrier family struck; one chip family serves single and bundle confirm (amends T15's composition rule).
- Keypad age and income edges handed to T07 as data.
- Nearest terminals auto-advance, never play `section_menu`.
- Any keypress resets the silence ladder.

**Count:** 42 − 1 merged + 5 added (`consent_notice`, `door_a_option_none`, `keypad_unknown_suffix`, `section_source_frame`, `results_widened_lead`) = **46 lines, 139 files**, plus the chip family (one file per box value per language; count set by T07's value lists). No interface moves — T17 made line ids manifest-resolved strings for exactly this.

- **Audio (T15, T17).** Renders 139 fixed files plus one bare chip per (box, value, language) at 8 kHz μ-law mono (`audio/<render_key>.ulaw`). **No `confirm_<box>_<value>` carrier family.** Day-one listening check: a chip after `bundle_confirm_intro` must not sound seamed at T15's 120 ms tail.
