## 🔬 Groq findings — three things that change later steps

**1 · The concurrency fear in map rev 27 does not survive contact.** The map priced the free tier
at *"6,000 TPM is one and a half phone calls"* — that was arithmetic against a full-history prompt.
The design that actually got ratified spends **two model moments per call at ~250 tokens each**,
so 8,000 TPM is **~32 model calls per minute ≈ 10–16 concurrent callers**, not 1.5. **The mitigation
was the right call and it over-delivered.** Nothing to change — but stop treating runtime
concurrency as the free tier's main risk.

**2 · The real cap is requests per day, and the pipeline is what will eat it.** 1,000 RPD per model.
A demo call costs 1–3. A Step 8 derivation pass over the full scheme corpus can cost hundreds in one
run, and a second run doubles it. **Step 8's throttle and its `data_cache/` are not optional and not
a nicety — they are what stops you arriving at 13 Sep with the day's quota already spent.** Re-runs
must be free.

**3 · `gpt-oss` are reasoning models, and the default setting silently returns nothing.**
On the first probe — plain call, `max_tokens=40` — `content` came back **empty**: the model spent all
40 tokens on a hidden `reasoning` field and stopped on `length`. Reasoning tokens are billed against
your 8K TPM and they are invisible in the answer. Two settings fix it, and **Step 14 must carry both**:

```
reasoning_effort: "low"          # required
response_format: {"type":"json_object"}
```

With `reasoning_effort: "medium"` + JSON mode, Groq returned a hard
`json_validate_failed` error with an **empty** `failed_generation` on *both* models. Under T18 that
is a model failure, and two of them drop the call to keypad-only. **`low` is not a tuning
preference here, it is a correctness requirement.**

**On D7 (the unverified latency budget):** T14 priced the model call at 300–500 ms against Gemini
Flash-Lite. Measured here: **518 ms and 573 ms** — just outside it, on one sample each, cold, over
home wifi. **This is not a verdict** — Step 14's 30-utterance bake-off is what reports p50/p95, and
D7 stays open until it does. But go into that bake-off expecting the 1.2 s response clock to need a
look, and time the bake-off from the start rather than adding timing later.
