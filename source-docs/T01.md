# T01 · Pick the telephony provider

**Type:** `wayfinder:research` (AFK) · **Module:** Audio · **Blocked by:** — · **Blocks:** T05, T14

## Question

Which telephony provider can give us an **Indian inbound number** that supports what the design needs, and what does each one demand from us before it will?

Must establish, per provider (Exotel, Plivo, Twilio-IN, Ozonetel, Knowlarity):

- KYC and paperwork required for an inbound Indian DID, and realistic queue time in days.
- Whether inbound calls stream **live media** (needed for barge-in and for ASR mid-utterance) or only record-then-post.
- How DTMF arrives — in-band events or a separate webhook — and the latency of that event.
- Whether audio can be played from a URL/file, and the codec and sample rate on the wire.
- Trial limits: can a trial number take real calls from a stranger's handset, or only verified numbers?

## Why it blocks

The provider decides the shape of the turn loop's I/O. Every latency and barge-in decision downstream is provider-specific, and the DID has queue time we cannot compress.

## Default if this stalls

Exotel — Indian-domiciled, built for this, already the earlier lock. Twilio +1 trial stays alive purely as a fallback demo path.

---

## Resolution — 23 Aug 2026 · **closed**

### Verdict

**Plivo, India data region.** The ticket's stated default (Exotel) is **overturned**.

Exotel demotes to fallback: better local support, worse paperwork, and a sales-mediated
onboarding we cannot schedule around a 12 Sep bar. Twilio-IN is **dead** — Twilio does not
sell Indian local DIDs at all. Twilio +1 survives only as the trial-demo path already named.
Ozonetel and Knowlarity are ruled out: enterprise sales motion, no self-serve signup, no
public streaming documentation to design against.

### The finding that decided it

The binding constraint is not features. Every provider here can do the turn loop. The
constraint is **who is legally allowed to hold an Indian DID** — and no provider rents one
to a private individual. All of them demand business KYC.

The providers differ in *which* business proof they accept, and that difference is the
whole decision:

- **Plivo** accepts a Business Registration Certificate that may be **either** a Certificate
  of Incorporation from MCA **or a Udyam certificate from MSME**, plus Business PAN or GST.
- **Exotel** asks for Certificate of Incorporation, company PAN, and address proof *in the
  company name*.

Udyam registration is free, paperless, Aadhaar + PAN only, self-declared, and issued the
same day — and a sole proprietorship uses the proprietor's personal PAN as the business PAN.
So Plivo's door opens with documents obtainable in an afternoon; Exotel's requires an
incorporated company we do not have.

**Critical, irreversible:** the Plivo account must be created in the **India data region**.
This cannot be changed after account creation. A wrong click here costs a full re-signup.

### Per provider

| | Plivo | Exotel | Twilio-IN | Ozonetel / Knowlarity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Indian inbound DID** | Yes, 022/080 landline series | Yes (ExoPhone) | **No Indian local numbers** | Yes |
| **KYC accepted** | COI **or Udyam** + PAN/GST | COI + company PAN + address proof in company name | n/a | Enterprise contract |
| **Queue time** | ~15 min–1 business day | Account-manager mediated, days+ | n/a | Sales cycle |
| **Live media** | `<Stream bidirectional="true">` over WSS | Voicebot applet over WSS | n/a (US only) | Not publicly documented |
| **DTMF** | `dtmf` event on the same socket | `DTMF` message on the same socket, **bidirectional only** | n/a | Undocumented |
| **Barge-in primitive** | `clearAudio` / `playedStream` checkpoint | `Clear` event | n/a | — |
| **Self-serve** | Yes | Partly — AgentStream needs enabling | Yes | No |

### Answers to the five sub-questions

**KYC and queue.** Above. Plivo review is 15 minutes to one business day for 022/080
numbers; the 140-series (promotional) and 160-series (BFSI) have separate, slower SLAs and
we want neither — HAQDAAR is inbound service traffic, so the plain landline series is correct.
Common rejection cause is a **business-name mismatch across documents**; the name on Udyam,
PAN and the Plivo account must be byte-identical.

**Live media.** Yes, bidirectional. Plivo streams live call audio over a WebSocket and
accepts audio back on the same socket, which is exactly the shape the turn loop needs.
Two attributes matter and are easy to get wrong: `bidirectional` must be lowercase `"true"`
(uppercase silently disables it), and `keepCallAlive` defaults to `false`, which hangs up
the call the moment the playback queue drains — fatal while the Engine is thinking.
`streamTimeout` also defaults short; raise it.

**DTMF.** Arrives as a `dtmf` event **on the same WebSocket**, interleaved with the audio
frames — not a separate webhook. This is the good outcome: keypad and speech land in one
ordered stream, so "keypad interrupts everything always" is enforceable at the socket
without racing a second transport. Classic `<GetDigits>` / `<GetInput>` XML remains available
for the keypad-only degraded mode, which needs no stream at all.

**Codec and sample rate.** `audio/x-mulaw;rate=8000` (telephony standard) or
`audio/x-l16;rate=16000`. Frames arrive at 20 ms. **Take μ-law 8 kHz** — it is what the wire
actually carries; 16 kHz L16 is upsampled nothing and just doubles bytes. Note Plivo's
content-type strings differ from Twilio's (`audio/x-l16;rate=16000`, not `audio/l16/16000`),
so Twilio sample code does not port.

**Trial limits.** No, a trial number cannot take a call from a stranger's handset — trial
accounts are restricted to sandboxed (verified) numbers. This is moot: **Indian numbers
cannot be rented at all until KYC is approved**, and approval implies a Standard account.
There is no trial path to the acceptance bar. Budget for a paid, KYC'd account from day one.

### Facts this hands downstream

- **T05** now has an exact sequence, not a research problem: Udyam → India-region Plivo
  account → compliance application → rent a 022 landline-series number. See the ticket.
- **T14** — barge-in is implementable: DTMF and audio share one ordered socket, and
  `clearAudio` is the primitive that discards the remainder of a line.
- **T15** — Mouth's ingest-time cache should render to **8 kHz mono μ-law**. Rendering at a
  higher rate means resampling 50 times a second on the hot path for audio the wire will
  downsample anyway.
- **Streaming costs $0.004/min on top of voice minutes** — noted, not modelled; cost is
  out of scope.

### New question this opened

Plivo enforces **media anchoring**: for domestic Indian calls the media must reside in
India, and a domestic leg bridged to an international leg fails outright. Whether piping
call audio out to a non-Indian ASR endpoint over our own WebSocket falls foul of that rule
is now a live question — and it constrains both the ASR choice and where the service runs.
Filed as **T20 · Whether call audio may leave India**, blocked by T03.
