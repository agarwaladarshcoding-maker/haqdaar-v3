# T09 · The filter table and what a survivor mask means

**Type:** `wayfinder:grilling` (HITL) · **Module:** Engine · **Blocked by:** ~~T06~~ · **Blocks:** T10, T11, T16
**Status:** closed 1 Sep 2026

## Question

What is the filter table, precisely, and what does `survivors = survivors AND mask` actually do?

To resolve:

- One box per eligibility dimension — what values may a box hold, and how is "not asked yet" distinguished from "caller doesn't know"?
- Does a scheme that is silent on a dimension survive every mask for it?
- CORRECTION handling: the undo stack, and whether a correction recomputes from scratch or reverses one mask.
- What happens at **zero survivors** — the case the flowchart doesn't draw.
- Is the table pure code with no model access?

Plus two items parked here by T08: whether the Engine is a bitmask AND or a vector search, and where the inbound keyword-selection leg lands.

## Why it blocks

The Checker is the only writer in the system. Its contract is the engine's contract.

# Resolution

## 1. The filter table

Built once per snapshot, frozen with it, never computed during a call.

For every (column, value) pair, one word with one bit per scheme. **A scheme's bit is set if its value matches, or if its value is `ANY`.**

```
mask[category = agriculture]  = 10100000
mask[state    = karnataka]    = 11011101
mask[occupation = farmer]     = 11101110
```

`ANY` sets a scheme's bit in **every** mask for that column. A scheme silent on a dimension therefore survives every mask for it. Stated, because it decides whether questions narrow anything at all.

## 2. Survivor state — masks are retained, not folded

`survivors = survivors AND mask` was the wrong shape. It is correct and insufficient.

Two standing commitments need a **number** per scheme, not a bit:

- The dead-end ladder reads "the nearest two" — and a set has no notion of near.
- The bar wants 2–4 named schemes — and if five boxes leave twelve survivors, an AND says all twelve are equally right.

Worse, a bare tally is also insufficient. Two schemes can miss one box each and be completely different animals: a health scheme that missed **category** is worth reading with a label; a Maharashtra scheme that missed **state** can never be had by a Karnataka caller and must never be spoken.

**Verdict: keep the turn masks instead of folding them away.**

Six answered boxes is six retained words. Three things derive from them on demand:

| derived | how | used for |
|---|---|---|
| **survivors** | AND all retained masks | the truth claim |
| **tally** | per scheme, how many masks hold its bit | ranking the near |
| **miss-set** | per scheme, *which* masks lack its bit | deciding what may be spoken |

Cost: six machine words per call. No accumulator, no score array, no new structure — just not discarding what we already computed.

**Vector search is ruled out.** It cannot produce a trace, and the LOG's second job (T04) is proving why each scheme was chosen. A ranking nobody can reconstruct fails the bar even when the answer is right.

This amends T06's "a turn is one AND instruction" to **one pass, one word retained per turn.**

## 3. What a box holds

- A box holds **a code, UNASKED, or UNKNOWN.**
- **UNASKED and UNKNOWN are the same instruction to the filter: no mask is appended.** They differ only in the LOG, and to T10, which must not re-ask an UNKNOWN box. There is no third mask; inventing one would be fiction.
- **UNKNOWN always widens, never narrows.** A caller who doesn't know their income keeps every income-gated scheme alive. Safe direction: the system never says "you are eligible", so a survivor the caller may not qualify for is a labelled read-out, not an untruth.
- **`category` is box 0 in the same table** — same rules, populated by the Router from the opening turn rather than by a question. It must be a box and not a pre-filter, because *"anything else?"* clears it while every other box survives, and you can only clear something that is in the table.
- **One code per box for 12 Sep.** Multi-value boxes ("I farm and I run a shop") are mechanically cheap — OR the two value masks into one turn mask — but they blur the miss-set, which is the thing the whole fallback rests on. The ladder forces a pick. Deferred.

## 4. Corrections — the question dissolves

With retained masks nothing is accumulated, so there is nothing to reverse.

- **The box vector is the only state.** Survivors, tally and miss-set are always derived, never maintained.
- A correction overwrites one box and everything re-derives. A handful of word lookups.
- **There is no undo stack**, because there is nothing to undo. The ticket's recompute-vs-reverse question had a false premise.
- A correction does **not** discard other boxes, even ones asked only because of the corrected value. The Engine keeps them; T10 decides whether any are worth re-asking.

## 5. Zero survivors

Retained masks make "widen the last mask" precisely definable for the first time — drop one mask, re-derive. But widening **by recency** is unsafe: if the last box was state, it manufactures schemes the caller is geographically barred from.

Two locks:

- **A scheme whose miss-set contains a hard box is never spoken** — not as a match, not as nearest, not with a label. This is the truth lock at Engine level.
- **Nearest-two is drawn from highest tally among schemes whose miss-set is soft-only.** If that set is empty, the honest dead-end fires with nothing read — a pass under T04.

**Widening ladder (supersedes rev 2's "widen the last mask and retry once"):** drop soft boxes one at a time, in an order T10 supplies, until survivors is non-empty or no soft boxes remain. Hard boxes are never dropped. Bounded, cheap, and it cannot speak a scheme the caller can never have.

The **hard/soft classification of every box** is the one thing T09 hands forward to T10. T09 establishes that the classification exists and that the Engine obeys it; T10 assigns it.

## 6. Purity and the interface

- **Pure code, no model access.** Ratified.
- **No runtime negation.** T06 pre-folds exclusions into a column's admitted set at capture; what cannot be folded becomes `gate_notes`, never spoken and never filtered on. The Engine applies no NOT masks, ever.
- **No numerics at runtime.** Numerics are stored exact and collapse to closed sets at build. The Engine sees categorical codes only — never a number, never a comparison.
- **The Engine is language-blind.** No language tag ever crosses its boundary. This lands T08's parked item: **Model** selects one keyword from the closed list, **Data** owns the list and the keyword→code map (T22), **the code is the interface.**
- **Out-of-set code:** impossible by construction, hardened anyway — treated as UNKNOWN, no mask appended, logged, handed to the ladder as a re-ask.

## 7. LOG contract (T16's input)

**The snapshot id plus the ordered box vector reconstruct the entire call.**

The mask table is frozen with the snapshot and the arithmetic is deterministic, so survivors, tallies and miss-sets at every turn are **reconstructible, not stored.** Survivor counts are stored as a cheap assertion that replay agrees.

This is what makes T04's "judged from the LOG alone" true rather than aspirational.

## What T09 hands to T10

T09 guarantees the information survives the call. T10 decides what to do with it:

- which box to ask next, given how much each candidate mask would narrow
- when to stop asking
- **which boxes are hard and which are soft**
- how to order survivors when there are more than four
