## Question

Nothing to decide. Provision the number and prove a stranger's handset can reach it.

**Done when:** compliance application approved, DID allocated, a call from an unrelated Indian mobile connects and reaches our webhook, and the recovery path is known if the number is revoked.

**Record on resolution:** provider, number, organization data region, credential location, queue time actually taken, monthly rental, per-minute inbound cost.

## Why it is parked

Two reasons, both settled 28 Aug:

1. **It blocks nothing.** The earlier body claimed it blocked T14; the map's ticket table has T14 blocked by T24 alone. Nothing in the design depends on a number existing. The Audio module builds against the Twilio +1 already in hand and against Plivo's documented event shapes.
2. **It proves nothing interesting.** That an inbound IVR can take a call is not in question and demonstrates nothing to anyone watching. The system's value is in what happens after the call connects — matching, reasoning, the LOG. Session time belongs there.

The original rationale for putting T05 on the frontier was uncompressible queue time. Plivo's 022/080 review is now automated at ~5 minutes, so that rationale is gone. What remains is a four-day errand, not a lead-time risk.

## The chain

Provider is Plivo, India data region ([Pick the telephony provider](tickets/T01.md)).

**Free — no billing is triggered by any step above the line.**

1. **Fix the business name string.** One exact string, decided once, copy-pasted into every field thereafter. Write it at the bottom of this ticket before starting step 2.
2. **Udyam registration** — udyamregistration.gov.in. Aadhaar + PAN, self-declared, no document uploads, no fee. Certificate issues same day. Choose a services NIC code. Download the certificate fresh from the portal — Plivo verifies it against government records, so a stale or reformatted copy fails.
3. **Get a rubber stamp made** bearing the business name and "Proprietor". ~₹200, same day. Needed for step 5; see The trap.
4. **Plivo organization, India data region.** Sign up at cx.plivo.com/signup. Region binds to the *organization*, not the account — a wrong pick is recoverable by creating a new organization from the console's organization switcher, no new signup. Still: pick India first time.
5. **Compliance application** — console → Phone Numbers → Compliance → Create Application → India. Upload **one** document: the Udyam certificate. Business PAN alone is not accepted and is not needed here as a second document. Select business type **Direct Brand** (we call for our own service, we are not a reseller). **The first application must be signed by an authorized signatory and carry a visible company seal** — so the uploaded PDF must show both. Plivo pulls billing address and GST details from this first application.
6. **GST status.** If not GST-registered, self-declare as GST Unregistered on the console. Payment for domestic voice must be in INR to Plivo's India entity — handled automatically on card payment.
7. **Approval** — expect a verdict inside 5 minutes. Nothing is billable yet.

**─────── spend starts below this line ───────**

8. **Rent a 022 landline-series number.** Monthly rental begins here. Not the 140-series (promotional only) or 160-series (BFSI only) — both need separate provisioning and neither fits inbound service traffic. Landline series is restricted to service and transactional calls, which is exactly what inbound scheme lookup is.
9. **Fund the account.** Plivo is prepaid; a zero balance can block service. Do this a few days before 12 Sep, not on the day.
10. **Take one real inbound call** from a handset that has never touched the account, and confirm it reaches our webhook.

## The trap

**The seal.** The first compliance application must be sealed and signed. A fresh Udyam proprietorship has no seal by default, and this is the step most likely to bounce a first-time solo applicant. Pre-empt it in step 3.

Plivo's stated rejection causes: registration cancelled/suspended/inactive in government records, document details not matching government records, expired document, an unaccepted document type (business PAN alone being the named example), a blurry or cropped upload, and a first application that isn't sealed and signed.

Name consistency is still worth holding — it propagates into billing details pulled from the first application — but it is no longer the headline failure mode.

## Media anchoring — and what it does *not* settle

Both legs of a call must originate and terminate in India; violations fail with `violates_media_anchoring`. This is a **PSTN-leg rule**. It says nothing about whether our WebSocket media may be processed outside India.

Do not let this be read as an answer to [Whether call audio may leave India](tickets/T20.md). T20 stands untouched.

## Recovery path if revoked

Part of "done when", so establish it while the account is fresh, not after a revocation:

- What Plivo's stated cause-and-cure is for a revoked Indian DID, and whether the same number can be recovered or only replaced. Note the console's **Suspended** application status is caused by unresolved UCC complaints and is cured by submitting consent proof — an inbound-only service should never reach it, but know the path.
- Whether Exotel remains a viable fallback given it wants a Certificate of Incorporation — if not, the real fallback is a second Plivo number under the same approved compliance application, which is fast because the KYC is already cleared.
- Twilio +1 stays alive as the demo path of last resort.

## Meanwhile — this is the live half of the ticket

Build against the Twilio +1 number already in hand. The provider difference is one serializer file behind the Audio module's interface: Plivo's `playStream` and Twilio's `media` events are not interchangeable JSON, and content-type strings differ (`audio/x-l16;rate=16000` vs `audio/l16/16000`). Do not let Twilio-shaped assumptions leak past the Audio boundary that T17 will freeze.

Parking T05 is only safe **because** that seam is honoured. If Twilio assumptions leak into Audio, the parked provisioning turns into a rewrite in September.

If international calling is ever wanted alongside domestic, it needs a **second Plivo account under a different email** — not a second number. Out of scope for 12 Sep; noted so it isn't discovered late.
