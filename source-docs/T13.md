# T13 · Dry-run the narrowing on real schemes

**Type:** `wayfinder:prototype` (HITL) · **Module:** Engine · **Blocked by:** T07, T10

## Question

Run the narrowing on paper — a spreadsheet is enough — against the real 50 and five real personas. Does it converge?

To make concrete:

- Five personas including at least one that should match nothing.
- Record the survivor curve per persona and the questions asked.
- Does it reach ≤4 within 6 questions, or does it stall at 12?
- Are any questions dead — never chosen, or never splitting anything?
- Is there a persona for whom the questions are absurd in sequence?

## Why it exists

Segment 01 says survivor counts are illustrative. Everything about the call's length rests on numbers nobody has checked. This is the cheapest possible check, and it needs no code.
