# T02 · What myScheme actually gives us per scheme

**Type:** `wayfinder:research` (AFK) · **Module:** Data · **Blocked by:** — · **Blocks:** T06

## Question

For a single scheme on myScheme, what fields actually exist, how structured are they, and what is missing that the call needs?

Must establish:

- The real field list, and which fields are prose vs structured values.
- How eligibility is expressed — sentences, or anything machine-readable.
- Whether benefit, documents and how-to-apply are separable, since the read-back needs exactly those four chunks.
- Language: what myScheme itself offers in Hindi and Marathi, and whether that content is official or machine-translated.
- What the raw scrape already in hand contains vs what would need a second pass.

## Why it blocks

You cannot design the scheme record until you know what the source can and cannot supply. This is the long pole's first metre.

## Default if this stalls

Assume prose-only eligibility, one language, and that every structured field must be derived by us at ingest.
